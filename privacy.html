StoryLodge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

StoryLodge is a reading app for you and your close friends. It's run by the StoryLodge team ("we"). This policy says what we collect, who sees it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address or your Sign in with Apple ID, your name, handle and bio. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your reading: books on your shelves, pages you log, start and finish dates, ratings, reviews, saved quotes, margin notes, lists and pinned books.
  • What you share: posts, comments, likes, books you send, clubs you're in, RSVPs and votes, and who your friends and close friends are.
  • Imports: if you import a Goodreads export, the app reads the file on your phone and saves your books, ratings, reviews and dates to your account. The file itself isn't stored.
  • Reports and feedback: what you send when you report something or report wrong book details.
  • Goals and settings: reading goals you set, which notifications you want, and who invited you, if you say.
  • Your phone, if you allow notifications: a push token that lets us send notifications to that phone, and your time zone, so reminders arrive at the right hour. Signing out removes the token.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or browsing history. We don't use advertising or tracking, and we don't sell or rent your information.

Who sees what

Every post has an audience you choose: close friends, friends, a club, or just you. Only the people in that audience can see it. Your close-friends list is private; nobody can see whether they're on it. Anyone signed in can see a short preview of your profile (name, handle, bio, how many books you've read and how many friends you share) so they can tell who they're adding. Your shelves, posts and ratings stay with your friends.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and everything above, handles sign-in, and sends sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, and delivers push notifications if you turn them on.
  • Open Library (the Internet Archive): when you search for a book or the app shows a cover, the search words or the book's ISBN go to Open Library, which also sees your device's IP address.
  • Wikidata: to fill in an author's country, the app sends the author's name.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can export your books at any time from “Settings”, then “Export your data”.

To delete your account, go to “Settings”, then “Delete account”. This deletes your account and everything tied to it straight away: your books, ratings, reviews, posts, comments, lists and friendships. Clubs you host pass to another member. Backup copies are removed within 7 days.

Children

StoryLodge isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
