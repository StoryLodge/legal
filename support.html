Common questions

How do I add friends?

On the Friends tab, tap the add-person button and search for their name or handle. They'll see your request on their Friends tab.

How do I import my Goodreads library?

On goodreads.com, open My Books, then Import and export, and tap Export Library. Save the file to your phone, then in StoryLodge go to Settings, Import from Goodreads, and choose it. Goodreads doesn't export start dates, and keeps only the last date for a book you've read more than once.

How do I start a book club?

On the Friends tab, tap New club next to Book clubs. Name it, pick a book and invite friends.

Who can see my posts?

Each post goes to the audience you pick: close friends, friends, a club or just you. You can turn Post it off to save an update without posting.

How do I report or block someone?

Use the ••• menu on their post or profile, or press and hold a comment. Blocking is quiet: they aren't told.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, Delete account. Everything is deleted straight away and can't be undone. To keep a copy of your books first, use Settings, Export your data.
